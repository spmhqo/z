A Content Journey, Step by Step

A strong piece of content guides the reader through a sequence of questions and connected ideas. Each stop creates a reason to move toward the next one.

1. Prepare the journey

ElementPurposePlanning question
IntentionThe reason to begin creating this content.What need or opportunity makes this content necessary?
Intent / DestinationThe change the reader should experience by the end.What should the reader think, feel, understand, or do?

2. Build the route

ElementRole in the journey
QuestionFinds the next turn: where are we, where should we go next, and why?
LogicBuilds the connection from the current position to the next one.
Supporting elementsMakes the connection visible, understandable, and credible.

Possible supporting elements

  • Examples and real events
  • Data and evidence
  • Analogies and comparisons
  • Counter-examples and explanations

3. Repeat the engine at every turn

  1. Current Position: Establish where the reader is now.
  2. Question: Ask where the reader should go next and why.
  3. Logic: Explain how the next position follows from the current one.
  4. Supporting Elements: Use evidence and examples to make the route clear and credible.
  5. Next Position: Arrive at the next idea, then ask the next question.
  6. Destination: Continue until the reader reaches the intended change.

Guiding rule: Let the content unfold through questions. Each answer should open the way to the next question instead of giving the conclusion immediately.
